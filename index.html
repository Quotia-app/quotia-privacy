Quotia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Quotia collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you set up in the app stays on your device.

Who is responsible

Quotia is an independent app. If you have any question about this policy or your privacy, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

What the app stores, and where

To work, Quotia keeps a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • your settings: topics, colours, font, app icon and delivery times;
  • the quotes you save as favourites;
  • which quotes you have already seen, so they don’t repeat too soon;
  • small housekeeping details, such as when the app was installed, whether you have seen the welcome screen, and a random number used to vary the order of quotes.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Quotia itself makes no network requests. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Notifications

Quotes are delivered as notifications scheduled on your device. Quotia asks for permission first, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Ratings and sharing

If you choose to rate the app, the store’s own review screen opens; anything you write there goes to the store under its policy. If you share a quote or the app, the text goes only to the app you pick, such as messages or email.

Backups

Your phone may include Quotia’s settings and favourites in its own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud or computer backups on iPhone), so they come back on a new device. That backup belongs to your account and is controlled by your device settings; we have no access to it.

Children

Quotia collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. Everything the app knows is on your device, and you can remove it by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If Quotia ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version of the app is released, and the date at the top will change.
